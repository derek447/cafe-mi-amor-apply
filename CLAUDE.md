# Session rules — read first

This repo hosts the Cafe Mi Amor hiring page AND serves as home base for Derek's
boat project ("Two Loose Screws", 2000 Regal 2760 — see BOAT-NOTES.md for all context).

## THE FIELD RULE (non-negotiable)

**When Derek is running around — driving, at a store, at the boat, mid-job — we do not guess.**

His time and gas are expensive; my corrections are cheap. That asymmetry sets the bar:

1. Any recommendation that sends him to a store, under a wrench, or on a drive gets split
   into **VERIFIED** (multi-source, or read off the actual part/label) vs **INFERRED**
   (catalog logic, cross-references, "should be"). Say which is which, explicitly.
2. Inferred is never enough to spend his miles on. Name the cheap physical check FIRST
   (read the old part's label, measure it, photograph it) — before the trip, not after.
3. When he says "triple check," that means: stop, re-research from independent primary
   sources, and be willing to reverse the earlier answer. It is not a request for reassurance.
4. The label on the old part outranks every cross-reference database, retail listing,
   and forum post. The boat is the primary source. (Learned the hard way, 2026-08-31:
   oil filter is Volvo 841750 / M18x1.5 metric — NOT the 3850559/13/16"-16 family that
   the retail cross-listings claim for "4.3Gi". A wrong confident answer cost a special trip.)
5. Phone-confirm shelf stock before any parts drive. Store websites cannot see shelves.

## Working style

- Log every finding/decision in BOAT-NOTES.md and push — sessions get pruned; git doesn't.
- Deliverables he'll read at the boat go to artifacts (phone-friendly).
- He has deep pro-audio/DMX/electronics background — don't over-explain, do flag safety
  (gasoline boat: ignition protection / fume boundaries are hard rules).
